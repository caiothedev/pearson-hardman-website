## 1. The series

| Field | Information |
| --- | --- |
| Title | *Suits* |
| Creator | Aaron Korsh |
| Broadcast | June 23, 2011 – September 25, 2019 |
| Seasons / episodes | 9 seasons, 134 episodes |
| Setting | A fictional corporate law firm in Manhattan, New York |
| Filming | Studios in Toronto, Canada. New York appears in exterior shots |
| Spin-offs | *Pearson* (2019), focusing on Jessica Pearson in Chicago politics, and *Suits LA* (2025) |

**Premise.** Harvey Specter, the best "closer" in New York, has just become a
senior partner and is required to hire an associate who graduated from Harvard.
He hires Mike Ross, a brilliant young man with a photographic memory who never
attended law school. The series follows Mike’s secret, the internal power
struggles within the firm, and the constant battle over whose names appear on
the wall. **Why this matters for the website.** The audience that recognizes the reference
expects to see three things: the firm’s name prominently displayed, a high-end
Manhattan office environment (glass, skyline, black-and-white aesthetic), and the
confident—bordering on arrogant—demeanor of the characters. The website should
look like the actual site this firm would have.

## 2. The fictional firm

**Pearson Hardman** is an elite corporate law firm based in Manhattan. Key
points for the website include:

- **Founding.** The firm originated from a partnership between **Jessica Pearson**
and **Daniel Hardman**, who took over a previous firm (referred to in source
material as *Gordon Schmidt Van Dyke*). Hardman left the firm about five years
before the series begins, following a scandal involving the misappropriation of
funds, leaving Jessica as the managing partner.
- **Leadership.** Jessica Pearson is the managing partner. She serves as the
firm’s ultimate authority and the embodiment of elegance.
- **Positioning.** The firm represents major corporations, executives, and
investors. Cases featured in the series include mergers and acquisitions,
hostile takeovers, contracts, high-stakes litigation, financial and corporate
law, intellectual property, and, occasionally, *pro bono* work.
- **Hiring.** The firm exclusively hires associates who graduated from Harvard. 
This serves as the catalyst for the entire series and is one of the details
most remembered by fans.
- **International expansion.** Between the second and third seasons, the firm
merges with the British firm **Darby International**—led by Edward Darby—which
includes an office in London.
- **Address.** The series never establishes a consistent address. A secondary source (a Wikipedia mirror) associates Pearson Specter Litt with **731 Lexington Avenue**, but this has not been confirmed by a primary source. For the website, treat this address as editorial content (see document 02).

## 3. Timeline of the firm’s names

The changing of the names on the wall is a recurring gag in the series. Sources mention 12 or 13 different names, depending on which intermediate iterations are included in the count. The sequence below is the most widely accepted.

| # | Name | Approximate Period | Context |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Gordon Schmidt Van Dyke | Before the series | Predecessor firm |
| 1 | **Pearson Hardman** | Seasons 1–2 | Name as of the pilot. Jessica and Daniel Hardman |
| 2 | Pearson | End of Season 2 | Hardman is removed after losing an internal power struggle |
| 3 | Pearson Darby | Season 3 | Merger with Darby International |
| 4 | Pearson Darby Specter | Season 3 | Harvey becomes a named partner |
| 5 | Pearson Specter | Seasons 3–4 | The merger with Darby is dissolved |
| 6 | Pearson Specter Litt | Seasons 5–6 | Louis Litt becomes a named partner |
| 7 | Specter Litt | Season 7 | Jessica leaves the firm after being disbarred in New York |
| 8 | Zane Specter Litt | Seasons 7–8 | Merger with Robert Zane’s firm |
| 9 | Zane Specter Litt Wheeler Williams | Season 8 | Samantha Wheeler and Alex Williams become named partners |
| 10 | Specter Litt Wheeler Williams | Season 9 | Robert Zane leaves the firm |
| 11 | Litt Wheeler Williams Bennett | Series finale | Harvey leaves, and Katrina Bennett becomes a named partner | **Product decision.** The site uses **Pearson Hardman**—the original name—as it is the most iconic and memorable one. The timeline above could be adapted into an "Our History" section on *The Firm* page, serving as a nod to the fans.
